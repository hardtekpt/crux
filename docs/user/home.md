# Home

Home is a dashboard of widgets that you choose and arrange yourself.

<img src="../../fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/phoneScreenshots/1_home.png" width="300" alt="Home dashboard with today's plan, this week, latest best, bodyweight and projects">

## Widgets

| Widget | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Today's plan** | One of your plans, with how long it takes, its blocks, and *Start session* and *View plan*. Until scheduling exists, Crux rotates through your plans by day of the week. |
| **This week** | Climbs and sends since Monday. |
| **Days on the wall** | Days you climbed since Monday. |
| **Latest best** | Your most recent personal best, with a star when it's new. |
| **Bodyweight** | Your latest weigh-in and the change over 30 days. |
| **Weight trend** | A chart of your weigh-ins. |
| **Sends per week** | A chart of sends over recent weeks. |
| **Sends by style** | Flash, onsight and redpoint, side by side. |
| **Recent climbs** | Your last few climbs. Tap one to open it. |
| **Projects** | Problems you've tried but not sent yet, with how many goes and when you last tried. |

## Arranging the dashboard

Tap the grid icon at the top right to start editing. Then:

- **Remove** a widget with its ✕.
- **Resize** it. Most widgets come in small (half width), wide or large.
- **Move** it by dragging its handle. Small widgets pair up in a row.
- **Add** one from the catalogue with *Add widget*.

Tap *Done* to save. *Reset layout* brings back the default dashboard.

The dashboard layout is a display setting. It isn't part of backups, and each data set (yours and
demo) shows the same layout.
