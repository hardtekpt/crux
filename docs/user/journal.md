# Journal

The Journal is one timeline of everything you've done: climbs, grouped by day and place; sessions;
training results; and notes. Newest is at the top.

## Reading the timeline

The panel at the top shows today: what you've logged so far and how the week looks. Below it:

- **Type pills**: *All*, *Climbs*, *Training* or *Notes*, each with a count.
- **Search**: finds climbs, places and notes by text.
- **Filters** (the sliders button): the period (last 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 12 months or all
  time), the result (any, sends, or not sent yet), places, and note tags. Filters combine, and the
  button shows how many are on.

Each climb shows its grade, name, style, goes and notes, with a colour bar: green for a send, grey
for an attempt. Tap a climb to edit it. Tap a session for its summary.

## Logging a climb

Open **+ → Log climb**, tap **Log here** on a place page, or **Log climb** inside a session.

1. **Where?** (optional): pick a place, then a facility or wall, then a problem. Your favourite
   places are quick picks at the top, and you can add a new place from here. Picking a problem
   copies its grade, which you can still change. *Save this climb as a new problem* turns a one-off
   climb into a problem on that wall.
2. **Boulder** or **Route**.
3. **Grade**: swipe the grade strip. It uses your scale from Settings, or the place's own grades
   (numbers or colour tapes) when the place has them.
4. **Style**: *Flash* or *Onsight* (first go), *Redpoint* (sent after trying), or *Attempt* (not
   sent).
5. **Attempts**: how many goes it took. Flash and onsight mean one.
6. **How hard it felt** (optional): 1 to 10.
7. **Day**: today is selected; swipe back through the last two weeks, or open the calendar for an
   older day.
8. **Angle** for boards, plus **Name**, **Notes**, a **photo** and a **video** (all optional).
9. **Log climb**.

Photos are copied into Crux and scaled to at most 2048 px, so they survive the original being
deleted. Videos are copied as they are.

To change a climb, tap it in the Journal. To remove it, use **Delete**; it disappears from your
journal, bests and charts.

## Notes

**+ → Add a note** opens the note editor. The first line is the title. A note can be **pinned**
(pinned notes come first) and have a **tag** such as *injury* or *beta*. Tags are stored in lower
case, so *Injury* and *injury* are one tag. Notes show in the timeline and on
[You → Notes](you.md#more-about-you), where you can filter by tag.

## Training results

Results you log on exercises (*You → Personal records*) appear in the timeline on their day, with
a trophy when they're a personal record.
